Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and who sees it.

Effective October 30, 2026. Plain language, no fine print.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, email, phone and mailing address.
  • Service and claim details: branch, service dates, date of birth, conditions, VA decisions and deadlines.
  • Social Security number, only if you choose to give it, so your team can fill out VA forms. It is encrypted before it's saved.
  • Documents you upload, such as your DD-214, medical records and VA letters.
  • Messages between you and your team.
  • Basic website statistics: which pages are visited and roughly where visitors come from. We store a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, not the address itself.

Why we use it

Only to help you prepare and organize your VA claim: reviewing evidence, filling out forms, writing statements and keeping you updated. We also use website statistics to improve the site.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • You, through your portal.
  • Team members working on your file. Access is limited by role and logged.
  • Service providers we rely on to run the site, only as needed for that service:
    • Railway (secure hosting and storage)
    • OpenAI (AI tools that read documents and draft text for your team to review; your files are sent for processing only when your team uses these tools)
    • Resend (sending emails such as sign-in links and notifications)
    • Stripe (payments, if you buy a paid self-service plan; we never see your full card number)
  • VA or others only when you ask us to send something on your behalf, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your file while you're working with us and for a reasonable time afterward in case you need it again. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, and we will unless the law requires us to keep it.

Cookies

We use cookies only to keep you signed in and to protect forms from forgery. No advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

  • See and update your information in your portal.
  • Ask for a copy of your file, or ask us to delete it.
  • Skip optional fields, including your Social Security number. You can write it on forms yourself.

Children

This site is for veterans, service members and their adult family members. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information we already have, we'll tell clients in the portal.
