CRSC & Combat Pay

Combat-Related Pay You Earned - And Were Never Told About

Combat-Related Special Compensation (CRSC) is a Department of Defense benefit - not VA disability - that restores tax-free retired pay for combat-related injuries. Many qualified veterans were denied, misinformed, or never told they could apply. We identify eligibility, correct denials, and build CRSC-ready packets in plain English.

  • Combat nexus proof is required - not just a VA rating.

  • Applies to regular and medical retirees.

CRSC FAQs
Is CRSC a VA benefit?
No. CRSC is a DoD program for military retirees. It is separate from VA disability.
Do I need a VA rating?
Yes. CRSC requires a VA-rated combat-related condition (10% or higher).
What evidence matters most?
Combat nexus proof: incident reports, awards, deployment records, and medical evidence.
Can I receive CRSC and CRDP?
No. Only one is paid at a time based on which is more favorable.
