How it works

  1. Tell us about your service

    Answer a short intake about your branch, dates, and the conditions you want to claim. It's free and there's no obligation.

  2. Upload what you have

    Drag your records into the secure portal: DD-214, medical records, letters, photos. Phone pictures of paperwork work fine.

  3. Get an organized packet

    We sort your evidence by condition, check it against the VA rating criteria, flag what's missing, and prepare the forms and statements.
