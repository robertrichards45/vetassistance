Security

Your records are personal. We treat them that way.

Here's how we protect your information, in plain language.

🔒 Sensitive numbers are encrypted

  • Social Security numbers are encrypted before they're saved.
  • Only your assigned team can see them, and only to fill out VA forms.

🛡️ Secure connections

  • Every page loads over HTTPS.
  • Forms are protected against forged requests, and sign-in is rate-limited.

👤 Only the right people

  • Clients see only their own file. Staff see only what their role allows.
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, never as plain text.

📋 A record of every sensitive action

  • Opening, downloading and generating documents is logged.
  • Departing staff are switched off right away.

What you can do

  • Upload documents through your portal instead of emailing them.
  • Never send your Social Security number by email or text, to us or anyone.
  • Use a password you don't use anywhere else.
  • Questions or concerns? Contact us. Read our privacy policy for what we collect and why.
