How an Intent to File works

  1. You tell VA you plan to fileOnline at VA.gov, by phone at 800-827-1000, or with VA Form 21-0966.
  2. VA holds your start dateIf your claim is approved, back pay can start from the date VA received your Intent to File.
  3. File the full claim within a yearMiss the deadline and your start date moves to the day VA receives your claim. That can mean months of lost back pay.

Not sure if you have one? Check your claim status on VA.gov or call VA. An Intent to File covers disability compensation, pension or survivor benefits, depending on the type you chose.
