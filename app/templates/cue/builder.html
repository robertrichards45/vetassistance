Before You Begin: Important CUE Information

A Clear and Unmistakable Error (CUE) is a legal challenge to a final VA decision.

CUE is not a “new claim.” It is a request for VA to correct a past decision because VA made an error that is undebatable, based on:

  • The evidence that was already in the record at the time of the decision, and
  • The laws and rating criteria in effect at the time of the decision.

New evidence cannot be used to prove CUE. If your argument depends on new medical opinions, new exams, or records created after the decision date, that usually supports a new claim or increase, not CUE.

A disagreement with how VA weighed evidence is not CUE. Saying “VA should have believed me” or “VA should have rated me higher” is not enough unless you can point to a specific legal or factual error.

This tool helps you organize information and draft a document, but it does not guarantee approval. You are responsible for reviewing the document for accuracy and submitting it to VA.

Not legal advice. If you have an accredited representative or attorney, you should consult them.
