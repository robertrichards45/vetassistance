How to Submit

Submit Online Using VA QuickSubmit (Recommended)

  1. Open VA.gov QuickSubmit and sign in (ID.me / Login.gov / DS Logon).
  2. Click “Start a new submission.”
  3. Choose the benefit type that best matches your issue (usually Compensation).
  4. Upload your generated PDF titled: “MOTION FOR REVISION BASED ON CUE – 38 CFR §3.105(a)”.
  5. Upload attachments (recommended): a copy of the VA decision you are challenging and any supporting documents that were already part of the record at that time (if you have them).
  6. In any notes field, paste: “This submission includes a Motion for Revision Based on Clear and Unmistakable Error under 38 C.F.R. § 3.105(a).”
  7. Submit and save your confirmation (screenshot or PDF).
  8. Return to your portal and click “Mark as Submitted” and upload your confirmation.
Checklist

Submit by Fax

  1. Print your CUE motion and attachments.
  2. Verify the current VA Evidence Intake Center fax number before sending (numbers can change).
  3. Fax the packet and keep the fax confirmation sheet.
  4. Upload the fax confirmation to your portal and click “Mark as Submitted.”

Submit by Mail (Certified Recommended)

  1. Print your CUE motion and attachments.
  2. Verify the current VA Evidence Intake Center mailing address before sending (addresses can change).
  3. Mail using Certified Mail / tracking (recommended).
  4. Save the tracking receipt and upload it to your portal, then click “Mark as Submitted.”
