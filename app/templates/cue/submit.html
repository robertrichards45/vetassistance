How to send it to VA

Recommended. Fastest, and you get a confirmation right away.

  1. Open VA QuickSubmit ↗ and sign in with ID.me, Login.gov or DS Logon.
  2. Choose Start a new submission, then the benefit type that matches the decision (usually Compensation).
  3. Upload the motion PDF, the VA decision you're challenging, and any evidence that was already in the record at that time.
  4. In the notes field, paste: This submission includes a Motion for Revision Based on Clear and Unmistakable Error under 38 C.F.R. § 3.105(a).
  5. Submit and save the confirmation as a screenshot or PDF.
