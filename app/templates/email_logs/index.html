Set up email (about 5 minutes)

Recommended: Resend. It's free for up to 3,000 emails a month and works on every Railway plan.

  1. Create a free account at resend.com.
  2. Under Domains, add vetassistance.org and add the DNS records it shows you at your domain registrar. This lets you send from your own address.
  3. Under API Keys, create a key.
  4. In Railway, open your service → Variables and add:
    RESEND_API_KEY = the key
    EMAIL_FROM = Veteran Benefits Assistance <support@vetassistance.org>
  5. Railway redeploys automatically. Come back here and press Send test email.

Already have Gmail SMTP set up? It keeps working if your Railway project is on Pro: set SMTP_HOST=smtp.gmail.com, SMTP_PORT=587, SMTP_USER, and SMTP_PASS to a Gmail App Password.
