Mission and tone
  • We organize and clarify benefits information so veterans can make informed decisions.
  • We use plain language and avoid legal or medical claims.
  • We do not guarantee outcomes. We explain options and evidence needs.
Standard of care
  • Document accurately and clearly. Do not speculate or edit original records.
  • Respect deadlines. Always confirm next steps and time-sensitive items.
  • Keep the veteran informed with short, actionable updates.
When to escalate
  • Urgent hardship, homelessness, or safety concerns.
  • Imminent deadlines, appeals, or legal representation questions.
  • Any hostile or threatening communication.
