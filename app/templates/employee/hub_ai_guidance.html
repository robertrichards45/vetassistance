How to use AI safely without replacing professional judgment.

What AI can do
  • Summarize documents and highlight missing evidence.
  • Suggest next steps and reminders based on patterns.
  • Draft plain-English explanations for clients.
What AI must not do
  • No outcome guarantees or legal conclusions.
  • No medical certainty or diagnosis language.
  • No edits to original evidence records.
Verification checklist
  • Confirm all dates and providers against source documents.
  • Check that recommendations match client goals and timelines.
  • Escalate unclear cases to Director.
