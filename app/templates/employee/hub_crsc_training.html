CRSC Training Modules

Use this guide to keep CRSC analysis accurate and separated from VA benefits.

Back to Hub Open CRSC Workspace
Module 1: CRSC Fundamentals
  • CRSC is a DoD program for retirees, not a VA benefit.
  • CRSC requires a combat nexus category: armed conflict, hazardous service, simulated war, instrumentality of war.
  • CRSC is tax-free and interacts with retirement pay and VA compensation.
Module 2: Evidence Standards
  • Confirm VA rating decision and code sheet are on file.
  • Document combat indicators (awards, deployments, incident reports).
  • Line-of-duty evidence or incident narratives must match the combat category.
Module 3: Denial Prevention
  • Do not label a non-combat injury as combat-related without evidence.
  • Clarify simulated war vs. training accident language.
  • Ensure narratives cite evidence and show how the injury fits the combat category.
Module 4: Packet Assembly
  • Use the CRSC workspace to verify the branch form and evidence checklist.
  • Generate a CRSC packet summary and a nexus narrative when needed.
  • Verify all documents are readable and labeled clearly.
