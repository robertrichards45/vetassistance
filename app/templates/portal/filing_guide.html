Quick Examples

  • "My condition got worse." -> Increase
  • "Here is a new doctor opinion." -> Supplemental
  • "VA ignored evidence already in the file in 1996." -> CUE

Hard Stops (Do Not Route to CUE)

  • New medical exams
  • New nexus opinions
  • "VA should have believed me" without a clear legal/factual error
  • Condition worsened later
  • Duty to assist arguments

CUE (Clear and Unmistakable Error)

CUE

Used when VA made a clear legal or factual error in a final decision. No new evidence allowed. Can result in retroactive pay to the original claim date.

Increased Rating Claim

Increase

Used when a service-connected condition has worsened since the last VA decision.

Supplemental Claim

Supplemental

Used when you have new and relevant evidence VA did not previously consider.
