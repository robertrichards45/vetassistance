TITLE: VA Rating for Knee Pain: Flexion, Extension and Instability
SUMMARY: Knees can be rated for limited bending, limited straightening and instability, sometimes separately. Here are the levels.

<p>VA can rate a knee in more than one way under 38 CFR 4.71a: for limited bending (flexion, code 5260), limited straightening (extension, code 5261) and instability (code 5257). Depending on the evidence, these can be rated separately for the same knee, and each knee is rated on its own.</p>

<h2>Limited bending (flexion, code 5260)</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Flexion limited to 60°: 0%</li>
  <li>Limited to 45°: 10%</li>
  <li>Limited to 30°: 20%</li>
  <li>Limited to 15°: 30%</li>
</ul>

<h2>Limited straightening (extension, code 5261)</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Extension limited to 5°: 0%</li>
  <li>10°: 10% · 15°: 20% · 20°: 30% · 30°: 40% · 45°: 50%</li>
</ul>

<h2>Instability (code 5257)</h2>
<p>Since 2021, instability ratings depend on the type of injury and on whether a medical provider prescribed a brace or an assistive device such as a cane:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>10%:</strong> a sprain or ligament tear causing persistent instability, without a prescribed brace or assistive device.</li>
  <li><strong>20%:</strong> persistent instability with a prescribed brace and/or assistive device (or, for an unrepaired or failed complete tear, one of the two).</li>
  <li><strong>30%:</strong> an unrepaired or failed repair of a complete ligament tear with persistent instability, where a provider prescribes both an assistive device and bracing.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Painful motion and flare-ups</h2>
<p>Painful motion of a joint can support at least the minimum compensable rating. The examiner should also estimate extra motion lost during flare-ups and after repeated use, so describe yours clearly.</p>

<h2>Evidence that helps</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Records of the in-service injury or symptoms, and treatment since.</li>
  <li>Imaging (X-ray or MRI) and any surgery records.</li>
  <li>A prescription for a brace, cane or other device, if you use one.</li>
  <li>A personal statement about giving way, swelling, stairs, kneeling and work limits.</li>
</ul>
